Brownie Dip

Description

A rich, chocolatey no-bake dessert dip that tastes just like brownie batter, made safe to eat by heat-treating the mix first. Perfect for parties, served with fruit, pretzels, or cookies for dipping.

Ingredients

  • 1 box brownie mix (15-18 oz)
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 8 oz whipped topping, thawed
  • 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips
  • 2-3 tbsp milk, as needed to thin

Steps

  1. Heat the brownie mix in a microwave-safe bowl in 15-second intervals, stirring each time, until it reaches about 165°F. Let it cool completely.
  2. Beat the softened cream cheese in a large bowl until smooth.
  3. Gradually mix in the cooled brownie mix until well combined.
  4. Fold in the whipped topping until the mixture is even and fluffy.
  5. Stir in the mini chocolate chips, adding milk a tablespoon at a time if you'd like a thinner consistency.
  6. Transfer to a serving bowl and serve with fruit, pretzels, or graham crackers.
