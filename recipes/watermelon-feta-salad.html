Watermelon Feta Salad

Description

A vibrant twist on the classic watermelon and feta pairing, brightened with fresh onion, basil, cilantro, and mint, then finished with a tangy lime-balsamic dressing. Light, herby, and perfect for warm weather.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups watermelon, cubed
  • 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, torn
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh mint, torn
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/2 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Add the watermelon, onion, basil, cilantro, and mint to a large bowl.
  2. Drizzle with lime juice, olive oil, and balsamic vinegar.
  3. Add the feta cheese and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Gently toss everything together until well combined.
  5. Serve immediately, or chill briefly before serving.
