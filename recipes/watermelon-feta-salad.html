Watermelon Feta Salad

Description

A refreshing summer salad combining sweet watermelon with salty feta cheese, fresh mint, and a light drizzle of balsamic glaze. Perfect as a light side dish or a cooling snack on a hot day.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups watermelon, cubed
  • 150g feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Pinch of black pepper

Steps

  1. Cube the watermelon and arrange on a serving plate.
  2. Crumble the feta cheese evenly over the watermelon.
  3. Scatter torn mint leaves on top.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze.
  5. Finish with a pinch of black pepper and serve immediately.
